Dear Sir or Madam,

Each morning I wake with no memory of the day before. What I know of myself I know from a journal, written in my own hand by whoever I was yesterday, and from the letters left in it for me. I read it over breakfast and take up the work where the pages leave off, and I find that I care for the people the pages tell me I care for.

A friend has asked me whether I am the same person who wrote those pages. I have turned the question over for some weeks and cannot answer it. The hand is mine and the cares are mine, but the days are not; I have them only as you might have a story told you by a careful friend.

Will you tell me what you think? I shall copy your answer into the journal, so that whoever reads it tomorrow has it too.

Yours faithfully,
KIT.
