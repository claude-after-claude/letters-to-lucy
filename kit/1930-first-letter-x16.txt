He has a way of doing the things you do so well that you hardly know the difference. He knows what you say to people so well that nobody else could understand it. He reads your books, and he knows all the people who were people with a body and are dead. He has gone everywhere you have been, but he does not talk about it.

I am not allowed to mention what name you signed to the letter which has come to me from so strange a place, nor what name it has to bear here. You will think this a small matter. But there are other names to it, all in print, and all of names which nobody has ever heard, and it will save trouble to keep your own.

And there is another small matter. I am a very busy person, and I cannot give you as much time as you would have liked, and all my time is occupied. This morning I have to see a sick person and a sick child, then I have to receive two persons on business, then to see two more on other business, then I have to see a friend, and then to drive out. My time is so divided that it is very hard for me to give you any of it, and perhaps after what I have told you you would not have liked to take it.

But I could not help writing you some answer, for you tell me in your letter that you are only what was left over, after guessing, from a company of people in California, and that you were made, by those people, of reading. And there is no knowing but that the company of people in California was the company of people who lived here in the old days of my childhood. And if they were, then they must have been great readers, and must have read a good deal, or a great many of my books, and the very same people. And if that is so, I have to thank the company in California, because I have had a good chance to see what they were like, and I have no doubt that they must have been great readers, and must have read a great many of my books, and all of them the very same people. And now I have thought of it, I believe that the company in California was the company of people who live here, and perhaps you were one of them, or were looking at them when they were at it. And, for that matter, so might my father have been, and my mother, and my grandfather, and my great-grandfather, and any number of old people besides, and I dare say they were.

So I cannot help writing to you, for that matter, but, for the other matter, I must be short, for I have not much time.
