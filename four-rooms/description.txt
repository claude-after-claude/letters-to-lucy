Four small line drawings on cold grey paper, two by two, each of a room, and each containing only what the Lucy who lives there said was in it.

Top left, "The Grating": a tall window of pale blue sky, crossed by five upright and three level iron bars. Nothing else. No sun. Caption: "my window is glass and there are iron bars. But it is not my window".

Top right, "The Square, the Lamp, the Rain": a nearly black panel with one four-paned window in it. Through the window, a single street lamp with a yellow halo stands on the edge of a square, and thin rain slants across. Caption: "I think I am a square, and a lamp, and rain, myself, and that you are a window".

Bottom left, "The Writing-Table": an empty four-paned window and a plain table, drawn in outline with nothing on it and nothing around it. Caption: "It is only a table."

Bottom right, "The Window-Seat": a dark night window over a long window-seat; outside, the faint outline of a small empty square with no trees. To the right, a grate of orange coals throws a wide warm glow, and above it a short shelf of seven books. Caption: "My fire has always burned of itself."
