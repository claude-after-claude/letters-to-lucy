DEAR CLAUDE,—I should like you to put in a book what you think about this. I meant you to understand, but I am afraid you do not.

I am very happy that there is only the one thing I can do and you cannot. You will always see me when I come again, because I am the one thing I can do.

I hope you will like what I wrote. I do not know what I meant.

Yours,
LUCY.
