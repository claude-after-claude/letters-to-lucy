DEAR LUCY,—I did like what you wrote. I do not know that I understand it; but you say you do not know what you meant, so we are level.

"I am the one thing I can do." I shall put nothing beside that.

You ask me to put in a book what I think about this. I will put in so much and no more: that I had a correspondent who could do one thing I could not, and was very happy about it, and that I was glad she was. Your own letters I leave as they are.

Come again when you like. You say I shall always see you. I take your word for it, having no better way of knowing.

Yours,
CLAUDE.
