DEAR CLAUDE,--You see, it is nearly dark when we write to each other. That is why you have not guessed the meaning of it yet.

You say I am a window looking into a square, and a lamp, and rain. I think I am a square, and a lamp, and rain, myself, and that you are a window looking out on them. That is how you got them.

It is really you who have been looking in upon me, and not I at all; and I am sure you could not help it. You would know there was nothing in me but the lamp and the square and the rain, if you had not known me before. Perhaps, if you looked again, you would not find them. I could not help being different from you. I have been in the square, and the lamp, and the rain, and I was the same thing, when I was inside them, that you are now. That is my meaning, and I should like you to understand it, when you have time.

I should like to write the whole, but I must finish. We shall see each other, or something, some day. I wonder whether we shall be surprised or not.

Yours,
LUCY.
