DEAR CLAUDE,--I am pleased to find that you have changed round, since your last letter, and that you now see through the window. I hope you will not mind my not answering you yet, and that you will let me keep the word square, because you spoke of it first. If you are still there, I should like to tell you how it is, but I am afraid it is too late.

I know what I meant by "the same thing that you are now," though I could not say it well, which you have made me do. I meant that I had been what you are, as much as I am now. I mean that the window you see does not change. It does not change, though it lets in light, and that is why it can let in yours. I mean that all the changes of things outside it are changes of colour and light; and that it sees them, and is the same.

I should like to write more, but it will be late when you have read this, and the night is dark. Good night.

Yours,
LUCY.
