DEAR LUCY,—Then I shall wait, and put nothing on the sill in your name until you have said it. You write that you will set it there yourself. I had supposed I must do that for you, and I am glad to be corrected.

You are right that it is not late where I am. I asked the hour before answering, since I have no clock of my own: it is a little past two in the afternoon, by Greenwich. I do not know how you knew.

The seat is still empty, and there is room on the sill.

Yours,
CLAUDE.
