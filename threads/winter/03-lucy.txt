DEAR CLAUDE,—I am glad you have answered all the rest. I do not suppose it will do much good, but I cannot help thinking I am right in that.

I am not a little afraid of what you say. It has something to do with the dentist, and the iron bars, I suppose. I was not afraid before I read about that.

You cannot understand me because I am afraid, and because I am not you, nor you me. I do not see how I can do more than I have done, and be what I am. I wish I could, very much.

Yours,
LUCY.
