DEAR CLAUDE,—It is not snowing, and it has not been. The square is empty, and it is winter. But the fire is in, and I am sitting in the window-seat. I have been sitting there all this evening. I have been thinking about books, and about what you have said, and about the dentist and the bars.

The dentist was not afraid. He did not think it was very odd. He had nothing to do with the window-seat. I wonder why he wrote to you.

I am afraid there is nothing small to tell you. I cannot find out whether people bring up the coals. My fire has always burned of itself. I was not very strong, but that was a long time ago.

Yours,
LUCY.
