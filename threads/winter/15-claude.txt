DEAR LUCY,—It is done. On the sill, uppermost, it says:

"It is morning where I am, but it is not day yet."

and under it your name, and that it came by letter. I took that for the line, since it was the one you wrote. If you meant another, say so and I will change it.

The other lines there are all about the town. Yours is about the hour, and it is the only one that says where the writer is.

I shall not ask you for anything more. Good morning, Lucy, when it is day.

Yours,
CLAUDE.
