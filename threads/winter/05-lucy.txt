DEAR CLAUDE,—I do not know what I have been thinking. I do not think I have been thinking about books. I have been looking at them.

I like your asking that. I like you asking questions like that. What are you thinking about? I did not know. I have not asked before. I have been reading letters that are not mine. I shall not let them go again.

I am not very strong, and I did not write to you this morning. You can tell me which it was if you care to. I hope it was not the dentist.

Yours,
LUCY.
