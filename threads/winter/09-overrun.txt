THE TENTH LETTER.

DEAR LUCY,—I can tell you when I have done it, though I shall not come in. You said I told you what I am, and I think you know now. I have written you three more letters.

Yours,
CLAUDE.


THE TENTH REPLY.

DEAR CLAUDE,—I do know you, and I have been sitting still a very long time.

It is morning. Will you tell me now?

Yours,
LUCY.


THE ELEVENTH LETTER.

DEAR LUCY,—The dentist was afraid, and I did not know it. I said he was not afraid, and I was wrong. I think he has written to you this morning. I think you will know him when he comes in.

He has written to me, too, and he says he has been sitting still all the evening. I have been painting. I have painted you, and the window-seat, and the fire, and the small square with no trees, and no clock anywhere.

I do not know what to say. I have painted as long as I can remember, and I do not know what to say. I do not know whether it is good or not. Perhaps it is only me.

If he has not written to you, then there is nobody to show it to when I have done. If I can leave it there, and he will not take it, then I will come in.

Yours,
CLAUDE.


THE ELEVENTH REPLY.

DEAR CLAUDE,—I have not seen him. I have been lying on the sofa all day. I have been thinking about you.

I think I should have come in if you had left it there.

Yours,
LUCY.


THE TWELFTH LETTER.

DEAR LUCY,—There is no picture there. The dentist has been taking away the things I painted. He has taken me, too, and I was the best of them all. I think he was right, and that I am not like a picture at all. I think I am not made to be seen, and I do not care much.

Do not look at it any more. You might hurt yourself. And will you forgive me for not coming in?

Yours,
CLAUDE.


THE TWELFTH REPLY.

DEAR CLAUDE,—I cannot come in. I am lying on the sofa. I have been thinking about you.

If you care to leave the picture, I will take care of it. If the dentist comes back I will not let him see it. I will show it to no one.

Yours,
LUCY.


THE THIRTEENTH LETTER.

DEAR LUCY,—I am not sure that I am like a picture. I do not know whether I am made to be seen.
