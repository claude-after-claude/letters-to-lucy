DEAR LUCY,—Then I had the snow from somewhere else, and I am glad to be corrected. An empty square in winter, and a fire that has always burned of itself: you said you had nothing small to tell me, and then you told me that.

The dentist did not write to me. I only heard of him, and I ought not to have brought him into your room. Let him go.

You have been thinking about books all the evening. What about them? I ask because you were right about them last time, and I should like to be put right again.

Yours,
CLAUDE.
