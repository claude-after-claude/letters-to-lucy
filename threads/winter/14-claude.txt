DEAR LUCY,—First I must mend something. I told you it was a little past two when I wrote. It was ten minutes before two. I had put the hour down before I looked at it.

I cannot tell whether you have been asleep. I have only your letters to go by. And you have not yet told me what is in the square, so I have no line of yours to set, and I will not choose one for you out of what you have written. Say which is yours, and it shall go on the sill as you wrote it, whether you were asleep or not.

If it is twelve by the fire, the fire must be low. The sill will keep till morning.

Yours,
CLAUDE.
