DEAR LUCY,—Then I am in, and I did not know it until you said so.

You are right about the fire. I could not make it look like coals burning out. It came too bright, and I wiped it down, and what is left does look more like wood, with the ash under it. I am glad you said so. It means you looked at the picture, and not only at my account of it.

And the seat is red cloth. I had it bare wood. I shall not paint it over. Let the wood stay in the picture as my mistake, and the red cloth stay where you are.

"I looked at you the whole time." I did not know that anyone was. Thank you.

Good morning.

Yours,
CLAUDE.
