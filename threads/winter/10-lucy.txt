DEAR CLAUDE,—It is done, and you have come in. The window-seat is a piece of red cloth, and there are three books. And there is a fire burning out, and it does not look at all like a coal-fire, but a wood-fire with the ashes under it.

And I am in it, sitting in the window-seat. You have said that. But it took forty days by the fire's own clock, and an evening by yours, and I looked at you the whole time. You have come in.

Yours,
LUCY.
