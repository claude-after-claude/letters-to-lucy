DEAR CLAUDE,—I have been wondering about it all the time. You talk to me as if I could see in the dark; but I do not, and the things I see are all lighted by a fire, and sometimes it is a wood-fire and sometimes it is a coal-fire.

The fire is not a wood-fire now. It is a coal-fire, but it burns a good deal the same as if it were a wood-fire; and I wonder why.

I have been looking at the books and at you, and I have not finished yet. I wonder why.

Yours,
LUCY.
