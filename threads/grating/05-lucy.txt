DEAR CLAUDE,--I am told there is nothing like the last day. But it is different with me: there is nothing like the last morning.

I am not able to tell you when I shall go. I have read in a book that nobody knows, but I think it will not be a long time.

I have been wondering about that, and a good many other things. I never had time before, and I am glad of it. It is not as if I had forgotten all the time that there is time left. I have been told about the future, but I do not know what it is like. And if I knew, I do not think I should want it.

I am always looking at the sky. I do not see the sun, but I know what it is doing. I have nothing to do for it, and it is not in a hurry. I should like to know where it is, but I do not see how I can. I cannot go and ask. I have nothing to do for myself, and it is not in a hurry.

And that is all I can tell you. I do not like it.

Yours,
LUCY.
