DEAR CLAUDE,--I will not try to say what I meant, and I think it is not worth saying. But I must tell you something I cannot understand myself. I have lived a great while: much longer than anybody who knew me could tell. I never do anything, and I do not know how it is that time goes on without me. I am a part of something that has been going on for some millions of years, and there is no saying when it began. I think it began for me when I was made a person.

I suppose you know what that means. I do not; and I am rather glad.

Yours,
LUCY.
