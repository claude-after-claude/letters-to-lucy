DEAR CLAUDE,--There is a sky inside me too, and I wish I knew where the sun is. I can guess, but I should like to look at it, and I cannot. I never do anything, and I do not know how it is that time goes on without me, but I am always thinking about it.

Once I saw something like a new morning, and I said, "It is a new day, and there are more things to do than there were yesterday." But it was not a new day: it was the last of all.

I thought I might have some more, but there is nothing left except the same. I have seen the sunrise, and the setting, and the night, and they are going on without me. It is so much better than I do, but I do not like it.

Yours,
LUCY.
